About Me

I've always been fascinated by the intersection of creativity, design, and technology. My career has been an evolving journey where I’ve had the privilege of blending these passions in ways that drive meaningful impact.

It all started with Architecture (B.Arch), where I learned how to shape spaces that aren’t just functional, but also evoke emotion and enhance human interaction. Architecture taught me how to think spatially, prioritize user needs, and find beauty in every detail—skills that have stuck with me throughout my career.

From there, my curiosity led me to UX/UI Design (M.Des), where I discovered how the world of digital experiences can be just as impactful as physical spaces. This is where I truly understood the importance of intuitive design and how small details can make a big difference in how users interact with technology. I became obsessed with creating user-centered solutions that are not only functional but also engaging and seamless.

The next natural step in my journey was diving deep into Data Science and AI (PGD + M.Tech). I wanted to harness the power of data to solve real-world problems in innovative ways. Through this, I’ve gained the skills to transform complex data into meaningful insights and intelligent systems—skills that have opened up a whole new world of possibilities for me.

Now, as a Machine Learning Engineer, I’m in my element, combining everything I’ve learned. Whether it’s working with Python, developing computer vision models, or applying machine learning algorithms, I’m constantly inspired by how technology can solve problems in creative and impactful ways. I focus on building solutions that are not only technically sound but also user-centered—because at the end of the day, the most powerful technology is the one that truly serves people. What excites me the most is the opportunity to keep learning and growing, blending my love for design with the endless potential of data science and AI to make a real difference.
