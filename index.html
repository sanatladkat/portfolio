03 THE PHILOSOPHY

Learn. Unlearn.
Relearn.

Jack of all trades, master of some, was never an insult. It's the only way to stay useful when everything worth knowing keeps changing.

It started with buildings: an architect's eye for how a room breathes, then an interior designer's eye for how a room feels. Somewhere in there, a camera showed up, and photography and videography became just another way of asking the same question: what makes a space worth looking at?

Then the tools changed again. Parametric architecture and computational design turned that same eye loose on code, drawing buildings out of algorithms instead of a pencil. Product design followed close behind: shaping things people could hold in their hands, and things they could only tap on a screen.

The latest stop is an AEC startup, teaching software to do something an architect usually does slowly by hand: read a floor plan and turn it into a full BIM model. Different tool every time. Same question, asked five different ways.
